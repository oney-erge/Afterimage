# Frozen protocol: second D4-style replication (D4R2), same design as the D4 replication

Frozen 2026-10-05, before any D4R2 measurement. It is a second, independent run of the D4
replication (`h65-d4-replication-20261005/`), same code, arms, plans, settings and analysis, on
fresh prompts and at a different time of day. Its purpose is to tighten the estimate of H6.5's
latency effect and to measure how much that effect varies between sessions.

## Reporting commitments (fixed now)

- **This run happens regardless of how the D4 replication ended,** and is reported regardless of
  its own result. It was not chosen because earlier results looked good or bad.
- **It replaces nothing.** D1, D2, D4 (Figure 6), P1, the 12 GB run and the D4 replication all
  stay in the paper. The five Llama latency studies are shown side by side with their dates and
  conditions.
- **No selection among runs.** If the result differs from the D4 replication, both are reported
  and the difference is discussed.

## Design (identical to the D4 replication; frozen plans)

- **Arms:** T traffic (`926b0adb…`), K greedy / profiled knapsack (profile `b88db4b5…`, runtime
  tier fingerprint `344a8f46…`), H H6.5 endpoint plan (`a31a0d4b…`).
- **Engine contract:** batch one, greedy, one output token, 8/10/12 GB limits, 16 GB host budget,
  0.5 GB margin, 4,194,304-element slices, prefetch depth 2, per-blob reads, decode-table reuse,
  decoded RAM tier, static execution. Per cell: fresh process, one warm-up token, cold page
  cache, 10 s cooldown, the archived gates, keep-awake, and a **120 s storage idle**.
- **Blocks:** 12, one fresh prompt each (`d4r2-prompts.json`, no overlap with any earlier set,
  72-110 input tokens, checked at preparation). Williams orders TKH, KHT, HTK, THK, KTH, HKT, each
  used twice.
- **Controller:** retries a blocked `status.json` write (the fix made after the D4 replication's
  stop). The assistant does not open status files during the run.
- **Limits:** 10-minute cell cap, 270-minute total cap. No reruns, replacement blocks, extension
  or outlier removal. A failed cell or token mismatch makes its block ineligible.

## Predeclared analysis

1. **Primary:** latency ratios T/H and K/H over all eligible blocks, and peak-memory ratios
   T/H and K/H. Geometric mean with a two-sided 95% Student-t interval on block log ratios.
2. **Secondary, predeclared:** excluding any block in which any cell's prefetch wait exceeds
   twice its arm's median. Also blocks 1-6 and 7-12 separately, and per-arm means.
3. **Pooled, predeclared:** the same statistics over the eligible blocks of the D4 replication
   and D4R2 together. The runs share a design but not a session.
4. **Also reported:** the stall rate, and the difference between the two runs' estimates.
5. An interval crossing 1 leaves the direction unresolved.
